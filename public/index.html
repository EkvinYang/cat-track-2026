Field record for Caterpillar fleets

Somewhere in your fleet, someone already fixed this.

Cat Track files every voice note, sensor alarm and repair against the machine it happened to. When the same fault turns up on another unit, the crew on that site gets the fix that worked, and Caterpillar engineers see the pattern across job sites while it is still a handful of reports.
